1. Executive Summary
The Travel & Reimbursement Portal (TRP) is an enterprise-grade web and mobile platform built specifically to reflect Simon India Ltd's (SIL) Interim Domestic Travel Policy (SIL/HR/Travel Policy-Domestic/2026, effective 13-Mar-2026). It consolidates corporate travel booking and expense reimbursement into a single system driven by a fully configurable backend policy engine — meaning that all entitlement rules, spending limits, city classifications, and approval workflows can be modified by authorised HR/Finance administrators without any code deployment.
Two foundational pillars distinguish TRP from generic travel tools:
Policy-Driven Engine: Every booking and claim is auto-validated against the employee's Impact Level, destination city group (A/B/C), and expense category before it reaches an approver.
Configurable Approval Matrix: Claims flow through a structured 4-stage approval chain — Reporting Manager → HR/HRBP → Payroll Team → Finance Team — each stage configurable in terms of routing rules, SLAs, escalation logic, and delegation.
1.1 Business Objectives
Digitise 100% of the domestic travel claim lifecycle for all SIL offices (CO.GGN, RO.BNG, RO.BBN, SO.ALL).
Enforce SIL travel policy rules at source — Impact Level entitlements, hotel room rent caps, food limits, city-group-based rates — automatically.
Achieve claim settlement within 10 working days (per policy clause 3.5: submission within 5 days, accounts settlement within 10 days).
Enable HR, HRBP, and Finance to configure all policy parameters from a backend admin panel — no developer intervention needed.
Capture GST-compliant invoice data for ITC reconciliation; auto-validate GSTIN on every invoice.
Provide real-time spend dashboards for Finance, HR, and Department Heads.

2. Backend Policy Configuration Engine
All policy parameters are managed through a dedicated Admin Policy Console accessible only to authorised roles (HRBP, HR Admin, Finance Admin). Changes are versioned, audit-logged, and take effect from a configurable date — ensuring historical claims are processed under the policy version active at the time of travel.
2.1 Policy Version Management
Each policy configuration is saved as a versioned snapshot with effective date and expiry date.
Active version is always auto-selected based on the travel date of the claim (not submission date).
All historical versions are archived and immutable after the effective date passes.
Policy changes require HRBP sign-off and are logged with the approver's identity and timestamp.

3.4 Admin Configuration Options for Approval Matrix
3.4.1 Stage Configuration
Add or remove approval stages (minimum 1, maximum 6 stages supported)
Rename stages (e.g., rename 'Payroll Review' to 'Accounts Verification')
Reorder stages via drag-and-drop in Admin Console
Set each stage as Mandatory or Conditional (e.g., HR stage only triggers if claim > ₹5,000)
Enable or disable parallel approval (two stages running simultaneously)
3.4.2 Approver Routing Rules
Rule type: Role-based (e.g., 'Reporting Manager'), Named Individual, or Department Role (e.g., 'HRBP for Business Unit X')
Routing conditions: by employee grade range, by claim amount range, by office location, by expense category
Fallback approver: configurable in case primary approver is unavailable
Delegation: any approver can delegate authority to a named colleague for a specific date range; delegation is logged and auditable
Auto-approval rule: Claims below a configurable threshold (e.g., ₹2,000) with valid receipts can be set to auto-approve Stage 1
3.4.3 SLA Configuration
SLA duration per stage: configurable in hours or business days
Business hours definition: configurable working hours and holidays calendar (company holidays excluded from SLA)
Reminder schedule: configurable reminder intervals before SLA breach (e.g., reminder at 24 hrs, warning at 36 hrs, escalation at 48 hrs)
Escalation target: configurable skip-level approver or role for each stage
3.4.4 Notification Configuration
Notification channels per stage: Email, SMS, In-app Push (toggle each independently)
Notification templates: editable HTML/text templates with dynamic placeholders (employee name, amount, claim ID, deadline, etc.)
Notification recipients: submitter, current approver, CC list (e.g., always CC HRBP after Stage 1 approval)

4.4 Reimbursement Form Auto-Population
Once the employee confirms extracted invoice data, the Reimbursement Form is auto-populated:
Each confirmed invoice line item is mapped to an expense category based on AI classification.
Amounts are grouped by category and compared against the employee's Impact Level limit for the destination city group.
Items exceeding limits are highlighted in red; employee must either adjust the claim or initiate an exception request.
GST summary is auto-computed and displayed: total taxable, CGST, SGST, IGST, grand total, and ITC-eligible amount.
Employee reviews the aggregated form, adds trip details (purpose, travel dates, advance received if any), and submits.

6.2 Advance Management
Employee can request travel advance before a trip through the portal.
Advance request routes through the same approval chain (Manager → HR → Finance).
Approved advance amount is visible on the employee's claim form and auto-deducted from the net reimbursement payable.
System tracks advance outstanding per employee with aging (Days Outstanding). Automated alerts:
Day 15: Reminder to employee and manager.
Day 21 (3 weeks): Flagged to HR and Payroll for salary deduction processing.
Payroll team sees advance deduction flag during Stage 3 review.
6.3 Policy Compliance Validation (Real-Time)
At every step of claim creation, the system performs real-time validation:
Invoice upload: GSTIN validated; duplicate invoice detected.
Expense category selection: Category eligibility checked against employee Impact Level.
Amount entry: Compared against city-group × Impact Level cap in real-time; red highlight + cap amount shown if exceeded.
Submission: Full policy compliance report generated and attached to the claim before it enters the approval chain. Approvers see this report at every stage.

13. Glossary
Term
Definition
IL / Impact Level
SIL's employee seniority classification (Level 1 to Level 6D) used to determine travel entitlements
RR
Room Rent (includes laundry expenses per SIL policy)
IE
Incidental Expenses — small miscellaneous expenses without formal bills (kaccha bills included)
HRBP
HR Business Partner — the HR representative assigned to a business unit at SIL
FH / Function Head
The head of the functional department the employee belongs to
3A Level
Minimum seniority level required for approval authority in SIL's travel approval hierarchy
Group A / B / C Cities
SIL's classification of Indian cities into three tiers for determining hotel and food expense limits
Twin Sharing
Mandatory room-sharing arrangement for Level 5B–6D employees when travelling with equivalent-level colleagues
GSTIN
Goods and Services Tax Identification Number — 15-character unique ID for GST-registered entities
ITC
Input Tax Credit — GST credit claimable on business expenses
GSTR-2B
Auto-populated monthly ITC statement under GST; used for ITC reconciliation
HSN / SAC
Harmonised System of Nomenclature / Service Accounting Code — product/service classification under GST
OCR
Optical Character Recognition — technology to extract text from scanned images
NLP
Natural Language Processing — AI technique to classify and structure extracted invoice text
GDS
Global Distribution System (Amadeus, Sabre) — platforms for flight inventory and booking
PNR
Passenger Name Record — unique booking reference for train/air tickets
IRCTC
Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation — entity managing Indian railway ticket booking
SLA
Service Level Agreement — agreed turnaround time for each approval stage
RBAC
Role-Based Access Control — access permissions governed by user role
DPDPA
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — India's data privacy law governing employee PII
